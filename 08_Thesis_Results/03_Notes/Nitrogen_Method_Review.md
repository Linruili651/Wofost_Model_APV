Because field-specific fertilisation records were unavailable, nitrogen management will be represented by explicitly defined scenarios rather than reconstructed farm practice. Crop-specific reference schedules will be established from regional guidance and published agronomic evidence. Reduced-input scenarios and alternative initial soil mineral-nitrogen conditions will be evaluated to assess sensitivity. Within each crop and nitrogen scenario, identical soil properties, initial conditions and fertilisation schedules will be applied to open-field and photovoltaic treatments. WOFOST 8.1 with the multilayer water balance and SNOMIN will simulate crop growth and nitrogen transport. Reported leaching will represent the cumulative downward nitrogen flux across the specified soil-profile boundary. The outputs will be interpreted as scenario-dependent estimates and will not constitute validation against field nitrogen-loss measurements.
