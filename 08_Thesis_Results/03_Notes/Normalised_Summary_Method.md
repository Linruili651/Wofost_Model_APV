# Normalised yield and seasonal ET

For each crop c and production mode m:

Relative ET = ET(PV, c, m) / ET(open field, c, m).

Relative yield = Yield(PV, c, m) / Yield(open field, c, m).

Each baseline is crop- and mode-specific. Valid open-field cases therefore overlap at (1, 1); they do not share the same original absolute yield or ET. Colours indicate crop, circles/triangles indicate PP/WLP, and filled/open markers indicate conventional/semi-transparent fixed-tilt PV. No jitter changes the numerical locations. Missing or unusable yields remain missing and are excluded from the scatter.

All modelled plots retain the same ground area. Ratios are calculated from area-weighted treatment-level outputs, rather than by averaging pointwise ratios. ET covers each run's simulated duration, so duration changes contribute to the seasonal ratios. ET is not irrigation demand. The original 12 crop-specific figures retain absolute quantities. The additional summary uses model results only.
