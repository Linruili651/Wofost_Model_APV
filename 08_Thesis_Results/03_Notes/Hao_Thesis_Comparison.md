> Nitrogen management provides a further dimension for agrivoltaic assessment. Berghuijs et al. (2024) extended WOFOST with crop nitrogen dynamics and the SNOMIN soil nitrogen module and evaluated the framework for winter wheat. Subsequent potato research examined nitrogen management under contrasting irrigation and fertilisation treatments (Berghuijs et al., 2026). These studies provide a methodological basis for extending crop assessment beyond potential and water-limited production.

> In an agrivoltaic modelling study, Rapella et al. (2026) examined interactions among fertiliser inputs, water availability and crop productivity using ORCHIDEE. Their results indicate that responses depend on climatic conditions and crop type. This supports investigating coupled water–nitrogen responses to shading, while the different model and study region limit direct transfer of numerical results to Aachen.

> The companion WOFOST 8.1 analysis therefore provides an additional scenario assessment of yield, water use, nitrogen inputs and leaching. Its estimated initial nitrogen conditions and specified management rules require separate evaluation. These results should be distinguished from the WOFOST 7.3 rainfed PP–WLP comparison and from the optimisation results reported in the present thesis.
