# Yield and seasonal evapotranspiration

Yield: t harvest-organ dry matter ha⁻¹. ET: mm, transpiration + soil evaporation.

Treatments: open field, fixed-tilt conventional opaque PV, fixed-tilt semi-transparent PV.
