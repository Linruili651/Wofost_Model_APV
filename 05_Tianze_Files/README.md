## Radiance (, 2026-10-19)



- `Three_Radiation_Notebooks/`: the three screenshot notebooks, unchanged originals, short-run copies, outputs, logs and usage notes. Start here for fixed tilt, vertical PV and single-axis tracking.
- `Original_Files/`: the two previously collected, older optimization-ready radiation notebooks, original weather and schema sample. They are distinct from the three files above.
- `Python_Adapters/`: exported older definitions and the older daily radiation bridge; not yet validated against the three newer output schemas.
- `Tianze_Defaults.json`: older adapter configuration.
- `Radiation_Requirements.txt`: unpinned optional dependencies for the older adapter. Consult the new three-model folder for its tested environment.

Radiation output is incident energy, not electrical generation or crop yield. New short runs and their limits are documented in the three-model folder.
