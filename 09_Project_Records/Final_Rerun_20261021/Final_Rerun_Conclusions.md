# Final rerun conclusions — 2026-10-21

The original final rerun completed all 36 WOFOST7.3 crop/mode/light-treatment cases, 18 WOFOST8.1 reference runs, and 18 managed water/nitrogen cases. Radiation was reused, not recalculated. All 36 yields reproduced the previous run. At displayed thesis precision, the main comparisons agree; one ET difference was a floating-point parsing tail.

Managed cases reached the common yield target in 15 of 18 cases. The three sugar-beet treatments did not reach the target. All managed cases had zero net irrigation under the specified weather and controller. This does not prove minimum water or nitrogen requirements or equal-yield savings. Maximum water- and nitrogen-balance residuals were approximately 7.82e-13 mm and 1.61e-11 kg N/ha. The experiments remain uncalibrated scenario estimates.
